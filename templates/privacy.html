Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles the information you share with it.

1. Introduction

Spendly is a personal expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly asks for and how it is used. Spendly is still being developed, so this policy describes the application as it currently stands and may be updated as features are added.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, Spendly asks for your full name, email address and a password. When you sign in, it asks for your email address and password. Spendly's expense feature is designed around the details you enter for each expense: category, amount, date and description.

3. How We Use Your Information

The details you provide are used to run Spendly: to create and sign in to your account, and to record your expenses and show you summaries of your spending by category and time period.

4. Financial and Expense Data

The expenses you log are information you enter yourself. Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts, cards or any other financial institution, and it does not move or hold your money.

5. Data Storage and Security

Spendly is designed to keep account and expense information in its own database. Because the application is still under development, specific security measures are not described here. We will update this section as they are put in place, and we ask that you keep your password private.

6. Third-Party Services

Spendly's pages load their fonts (DM Serif Display and DM Sans) from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore requests those font files directly from Google when you visit Spendly. Spendly does not otherwise include advertising, analytics or other third-party services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Spendly does not yet have a feature for deleting your account or a defined retention period. When these are added, this section will explain how they work.

8. User Privacy Rights

You decide what information you enter into Spendly and may choose not to provide it. If you would like to ask about, correct or remove information associated with you, please get in touch using the contact details below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as Spendly changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch with the Spendly team. Contact details will be added here.
